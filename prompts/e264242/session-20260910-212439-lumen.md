## Session summary

**Delivered:** a working simulator at
https://lumen-pricing-case-template-nine.vercel.app — 7 merged pull requests,
one branch per module, 30 passing tests, all 12 data-room exhibits in use.

**Three things the model changed our minds about**, each caught by checking
output against reality rather than by accepting the first plausible answer:

1. A 1% year-one market share sounds modest but implied roughly ten times
   LUMEN's entire existing business. Recalibrated to 0.1%.
2. "Cheap price through mass retail is the CFO-friendly option" is wrong in
   this data. Retail/Grocery has the weakest per-unit contribution at every
   tested price, and total profit peaks near €2.19 — so both the finance and
   brand readings prefer the same channels and disagree only about price.
3. German consumers' own stated acceptable range (€1.38–€1.97) sits below
   the profit-maximising price. The tool shows both rather than picking the
   convenient one.
